# Security Policy

This repository is a curated catalog of links and projects. Do not submit malicious URLs, compromised projects, credentials, personal data, or operational payloads.

Report a suspected malicious or compromised entry by email to `frangelrcbarrera@gmail.com` with the subject `[SECURITY] awesome-ai-agents`. Do not publish exploit details in an issue. Include a sanitized URL or project reference and the reason for review. GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting is not assumed to be enabled for this repository.

Entries may be removed or corrected when their links fail, their ownership changes, or their content no longer meets the catalog’s authorized-use and safety expectations.
